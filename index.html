Hackathon build · Hackcache ’26

Saral Banking

An age-adaptive banking web app with three modes: a simplified experience for senior citizens, everyday banking for adults and a young saver account for teens.

  • Read-aloud with the Web Speech API, large text, high-contrast mode, and full English and Hindi support.
  • Scam-awareness tools: fraud alerts, a safety quiz and cybercrime helpline links.
  • Parent-set limits and savings goals for young users.
  • State managed with React Context and hooks; preferences saved in localStorage.

OpportunityTracker

A placement platform that reads your resume, gives it an ATS score and recommends internships that match your skills.

  • Parses PDF, DOCX and TXT resumes with PDF.js and matches skills against job requirements.
  • Dashboard with live metrics, job search and sorting, bookmarks and side-by-side job comparison.
  • Node.js and Express login with bcrypt password hashing, JWT cookies and rate limiting.
